mart Travel Plan & Discovery
Discover places. Plan your days. Travel with everything in one place.

Smart Travel Plan & Discovery is a personal travel-planning app that brings destination inspiration, trip itineraries, packing lists, budgets, and useful travel information into a single workspace. Explore where to go, shape a trip around your interests, and keep the details organized before you leave.
What you can do
- Discover destinations: Browse curated travel ideas, explore destination details, search for places, and save destinations that catch your eye.
- Create your own trips: Choose a destination, travel dates, number of travelers, interests, and preferred travel pace.
- Build day-by-day itineraries: Add, edit, and reorder activities, set times and durations, and keep notes for each day.
- Explore places on a map: View saved locations and available walking or driving routes alongside your itinerary.
- Get planning suggestions: Review optional AI-assisted itinerary proposals before choosing whether to apply them to your plan.
- Stay organized: Manage trip notes, travel checklists, packing lists, and ready-to-use packing templates; track what you've completed.
- Plan your spending: Estimate transport, food, accommodation, and activities using adjustable costs. Add custom expenses, record actual spending, and compare it with your budget.
- Check travel essentials: View on-demand weather forecasts for eligible travel dates and use clearly labeled, dated currency reference rates.
- Keep a copy of your plans: Save trips in your browser, export or import a trip backup, and print a travel summary.
How it works
1. Find inspiration. Explore destinations or search for a place you want to visit.
2. Start a trip. Set your dates, travelers, interests, and pace.
3. Shape each day. Add activities, arrange your schedule, and review places on the map.
4. Prepare for the journey. Build packing and travel checklists, estimate costs, and check the weather when available.
5. Keep everything together. Return to your saved trip, track expenses, print a summary, or export a backup.
Good to know
No account required. The current experience is designed for guest planning. Your saved trips and related information remain in the browser on the device where you created them. They do not automatically sync across devices, so export a backup before clearing browser data or switching browsers.

Some information depends on external services. Destination search, maps, routes, optional AI suggestions, and weather may require an internet connection or configured services. Availability can vary, and some live integrations are still being verified.

Travel estimates are guides, not quotations. Budget defaults are editable examples, currency references are static rather than live exchange rates, and weather forecasts are available only for supported dates. The app does not book travel or process payments.
Make room for the journey, not just the itinerary.
